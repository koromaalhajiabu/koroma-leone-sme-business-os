How the scouting portal works

01 · Club approval

Club managers create player profiles from their existing squad records and approve the public information.

02 · Consent first

Players must have permission recorded. Under-18 profiles also require guardian consent and use a restricted public identity.

03 · Scout verification

Scouts create an account, confirm their email and await Koroma Leone Tech platform verification.

04 · Club enquiry

Verified scouts send enquiries to the club. The club responds through the SME Business OS and the scout receives an in-app notification.

This portal is a discovery and introduction service. It does not guarantee trials, contracts, transfers, representation or player eligibility. Clubs and scouts remain responsible for checking identities, registration status, safeguarding and applicable football regulations.
