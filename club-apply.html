Application process

The club's authorised representative must submit the application using their own email account.
1
Create or sign into an account

Confirm the email address used for the application.

2
Submit club information

Provide club identity, manager details and evidence of authority.

3
Developer review

We may approve, reject or request additional information.

4
Workspace and website setup

Approval creates a club-owned workspace and a verified website draft.

5
Manager controls publishing

You decide whether Koroma Leone Tech can publish directly or must submit changes for your approval.

Verification note

The verification badge means the applicant and club information were reviewed by Koroma Leone Tech. It is not FIFA, SLFA, league or government accreditation. The platform may request further evidence before approval.

Account access

Use the same Koroma Leone Tech account for this application and the club manager workspace. You do not need to share your password with the developer.

Club application

Complete all required fields. Upload one supporting PDF or image if available. Your evidence is stored privately and is not displayed on the public club website.
