CLUB MANAGER ROADMAP

Take your club public and connect with scouts

Follow these stages to move from club registration to a published website, player discovery profiles and professional scout enquiries.

01

Create your club account and apply

Use the club's authorised representative's email, confirm it, then submit the club details and proof of authority. Koroma Leone Tech reviews the application before a workspace is created.

Start club application ↗
02

Open your private club workspace

After approval, sign in with the same account and open Club Website Studio. Your club workspace is separate from other clubs.

Open club manager workspace ↗
03

Build and publish your official website

In Club Website Studio, complete Club Identity, Website Pages, News, Staff, Fixtures, Gallery and Sponsors. Preview the site, then use Overview → Publish Website. Copy Public Link only after publication.

View public club directory ↗
04

Make your players discoverable

Add squad records, create Scout Profiles and publish only information approved by the player. Under-18 profiles need player and guardian consent and use a restricted public identity. Do not publish private phone numbers or personal email addresses.

Open player discovery ↗
05

Connect with verified scouts

Scouts register through the International Scouting Portal, confirm their email and wait for platform verification. Verified scouts can send formal enquiries to a club; managers review and respond in Club Website Studio → Scouting Enquiries.

Open international scouting portal ↗
06

Share opportunities worldwide

Share the published club website link through WhatsApp, social media, club announcements and football networks. Eligible adult players with active consent can also be listed for transfer, loan, trial or free-agent opportunities.

View player opportunity market ↗

How scout contact works: verified scouts can submit enquiries through a player profile. The enquiry is routed to the club, not directly to a player's private contact details. You can also contact scouts listed in the portal when they have chosen to display a public contact email. Platform verification is an internal Koroma Leone Tech review, not FIFA, SLFA or government accreditation. Public publishing improves discoverability but does not guarantee that an international scout will view or respond to a profile.
Access and safeguarding: Club workspaces must remain isolated from one another. Public player information is limited by club publication settings and player consent. Under-18 players are subject to additional privacy protections and are not listed for transfer, loan or trial. Platform verification is an internal Koroma Leone Tech review, not FIFA or national-association accreditation. This platform does not guarantee trials, contracts, transfers or representation.
